Karmi Laven ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Who is responsible for your data

Karmi Laven is operated by Melbe Home UK Ltd, registered in England and Wales, company number 15549022. Registered office: 8 Turner Court, High Street, Berkhamsted, HP4 3ZE. Melbe Home UK Ltd is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. For any privacy question or request, email hello@karmilaven.com.

What we collect

  • Order details: name, email, phone number, delivery address, and items purchased.
  • Where your visit came from (for example a campaign link), recorded with your order.
  • If you write a review: your rating and review, and if you add them, your name, email, order number and a photo.
  • If you join our email list: your email address.
  • Payment is processed securely by Stripe; we do not store your card details.
  • Basic site analytics (pages visited) to improve our store.

How we use it

  • To process and dispatch your order and provide customer support.
  • To send order confirmations and, if you opt in, occasional updates.

Sharing

We only share data with the providers needed to run the store (e.g. Stripe for payments, Royal Mail for delivery, Resend for emails). We never sell your data.

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use the following technologies to measure and improve our store:

  • Meta Pixel (Facebook/Instagram): We use the Meta Pixel (ID 1791961201982687) to measure ad performance and understand how visitors interact with our site. This sends anonymised event data (page views, add-to-cart, purchases) to Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. When you complete a purchase we also send the purchase to Meta from our server, with your email address and phone number in coded (hashed) form, only if you accepted cookies. The Pixel is only loaded after you give consent. For more information, see Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics (GA4): If activated, we use GA4 to measure site traffic and conversions. Data is processed by Google LLC under their Privacy Policy. GA4 is only loaded with your consent.
  • Email (Resend): If you sign up for email updates, we keep your email address in our own customer database (hosted by Supabase) and send our emails through Resend (Resend, Inc.), which uses your address only to deliver them. Order emails (confirmation and dispatch) are sent the same way. We do not share your browsing activity with our email provider. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time with the link in any of them, or by emailing hello@karmilaven.com.
  • Stripe: Payment processing by Stripe Payments Europe Ltd. Stripe may set cookies for fraud prevention. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.

You can manage your cookie preference at any time by clicking "Reject" in the cookie banner that appears when you first visit our site. Your choice is stored in your browser and can be cleared by clearing browser storage.

Where your data is held

Our website and its order, sign-up and review functions run on Vercel (hosting). Our customer database (orders, email list, reviews and review photos) is hosted by Supabase. Our own team uses it to run the store.

International transfers

Some of the providers we use (Stripe, Resend, Vercel, Supabase, Meta and Google) are based in, or may process data in, countries outside the UK, including the United States. When your data is transferred to the US, we rely on the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (the "UK-US data bridge") where the provider is certified under it, or otherwise on the ICO's International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA) or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. For other countries we rely on UK adequacy regulations or these same contract terms. Email us for more detail.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to data portability. Where we rely on your consent (marketing emails, cookies, reviews) you can withdraw it at any time; this does not affect what we did before. You can object to direct marketing at any time and we will stop. You may request any of these by emailing hello@karmilaven.com. We aim to reply within one month. Please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
