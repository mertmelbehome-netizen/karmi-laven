Your questions, answered

Are you online only?

No — Karmi Laven is part of Melbe Home UK Ltd, an established UK retailer with physical shops in Watford and Guildford. karmilaven.com is our online home for the soft gradient bracelet collection; the same brand, the same crystals, online and in store.

Where are your shops?

You'll find Melbe Home in Watford (The Harlequin) and Guildford (White Lion Walk). Pop in and see the full range in person.

Where are the bracelets made?

Each bracelet is hand-strung in Turkey using crystals selected for colour accuracy and shine. We're a UK brand — designed, bought, and sold here — and we're proud of the craft behind every piece.

How do multi-buy savings work?

The more you add to your bag, the less each bracelet costs — 2 for £5.50 · 3 for £7.50 · 6 for £14.99. Savings are applied automatically at checkout. Mix any colours you like.

When will my order arrive?

We ship from our Watford shop within 1–2 business days. Standard UK delivery takes 2–5 days. £1.49 UK delivery, free from £12 (or 5 items).

Can I return or exchange?

Yes — you can cancel your order within 14 days of receiving it, then send the item back within 14 days of telling us. Christmas returns: orders placed from 1 December 2026 can be returned until 15 January 2027, or within your standard 14-day period if that ends later. This is in addition to, and does not affect, your statutory right to cancel. Please return items unused and in their original packaging where you can. If an item has been handled more than is needed to check it, we may reduce your refund by the amount it has lost in value. See our Shipping & Returns page for full details.
