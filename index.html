Good to know

Your questions, answered

Are you online only?

No — Karmi Laven is part of Melbe Home UK Ltd, an established UK retailer with physical shops in Watford and Guildford. karmilaven.com is our online home for the soft gradient bracelet collection; the same brand, the same pieces, online and in store.

Where are your shops?

You'll find Melbe Home in Watford (The Harlequin) and Guildford (White Lion Walk). Pop in and see the full range in person.

Where are the bracelets made?

Each bracelet is made in Turkey from faceted beads selected for colour accuracy and shine. We're a UK brand — chosen, bought and sold here — and we're proud of the craft behind every piece.

How does 70% off when you buy any 10+ items work?

Prices are £5, £8 or £13 per piece depending on the line (shown on each item). Put any 10 or more items in your bag (any mix) and you get 70% off every item: £1.50, £2.40 or £3.90 each, with free UK delivery. Under 10 items, UK delivery is £1.49. The 10+ price is applied automatically in your bag; promo codes can’t be added to it.

When will my order arrive?

We ship from our Watford shop within 1–2 business days. Standard UK delivery takes 2–5 days. £1.49 UK delivery · free with any 10+ items.

Can I return or exchange?

Yes — you can cancel your order within 14 days of receiving it, then send the item back within 14 days of telling us. Christmas returns: orders placed from 1 December 2026 can be returned until 15 January 2027, or within your standard 14-day period if that ends later. This is in addition to, and does not affect, your statutory right to cancel. Please return items unused and in their original packaging where you can. If an item has been handled more than is needed to check it, we may reduce your refund by the amount it has lost in value. See our Shipping & Returns page for full details.
