Shipping & Returns

Updated November 2026

Shipping

We ship across the United Kingdom via Royal Mail.

  • UK standard delivery — £1.49. Free UK delivery on orders of £12 or more.
  • Estimated delivery 2–5 business days after dispatch.
  • Orders ship within 1–2 business days.
  • You will receive a confirmation email once your order is dispatched.

Returns & Refunds

We want you to love your Karmi Laven piece. If it isn't right, you can change your mind: you may cancel your order within 14 days of the day you receive it, and you then have a further 14 days from telling us to send the item back.

Christmas returns: orders placed from 1 December 2026 can be returned until 15 January 2027, or within your standard 14-day period if that ends later. This is in addition to, and does not affect, your statutory right to cancel.

  • Please return items unused and in their original packaging where you can. If an item has been handled more than is needed to check it, we may reduce your refund by the amount it has lost in value.
  • To cancel or start a return, email hello@karmilaven.com with your order number.
  • Return postage is the customer's responsibility unless the item is faulty.
  • We will refund you within 14 days of receiving the item back or of receiving proof that you have sent it back, whichever is earlier. If you cancel your whole order, your refund includes the original standard delivery charge.
  • If you cancel before we have dispatched your order, we will refund you within 14 days of the day you told us.

Faulty items

If an item is faulty, tell us as soon as you can. You can reject a faulty item within 30 days for a full refund; after that you may be entitled to a repair or replacement, and if that does not fix it, a price reduction or refund.
